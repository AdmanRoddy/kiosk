Rates

Field labor rate is what one hour of field time really costs Adman — wages plus burden. It is the price tag on every hour the shop saves the field. Shop cost comes from each man's own rate on the Crew tab, so you never set it here.

Nobody is invoiced, so there is no sell rate, overhead or profit to set. The only question this answers is: did the shop cost less than the field would have.

Field hours saved — unit ratesWhat the field would have burned on work the shop did

Read a row as "field hours saved per per unit". Wire is 2 hours per 1000 ft; an MC whip with a device is 0.616 hours each.
